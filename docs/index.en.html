SHOTBOOK

An app for people who shoot on a smartphone and want to understand how a shot they like was made.

SHOTBOOK offers a curated catalog of reference photos with suggested smartphone camera settings and a camera guide for about 50 phone models. You can save the photos you like, and on the “Repeat this shot” screen you can overlay a reference photo on the live camera preview to take a similar shot. Photos taken on that screen are saved only to the gallery on your device and are never uploaded.

With an optional account you can publish your own photos to the community feed together with a description and the settings you used. The app has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Available for Android and iOS. Developer: Ochizzz Asakivenz.
