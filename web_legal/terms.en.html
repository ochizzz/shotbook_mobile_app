1. Age

To create an account and publish photos, you must be at least 13 or the minimum age required in your country (16 in some EEA countries). If you are under that age, do not create an account.

2. Your account

  • An account is optional. You need one to publish photos, keep a profile and store your saved photos on the server.
  • Use a working email address and keep your password secret. You are responsible for everything that happens in your account.
  • One account per person. Do not share your account or use someone else’s.
  • Your username, display name, bio and avatar are also subject to the community guidelines. They must not mislead, insult, or impersonate another person or organisation.

3. What the app offers

The app includes a catalog of reference photos with suggested camera settings, a smartphone camera guide, the “Repeat this shot” screen and a community feed. The app is free. We may change, add or remove features, and may suspend or discontinue the service temporarily or permanently. We will try to give advance notice of significant changes that affect your data.

Authors are credited in the app. These photos belong to their authors; if you want to use one outside the app, follow the terms of its licence. The camera settings shown with a photo are our recommendations, not the photo’s original EXIF data.

6. Community guidelines

SHOTBOOK is a community about photography. We do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behaviour: violations lead to removal of content and blocking of the account. You must not post or share:

  • nudity, pornography or sexual content;
  • any content that sexualises minors — no exceptions;
  • violence, cruelty, blood and gore, animal abuse, or encouragement of violence or self-harm;
  • bullying, insults, threats, harassment or humiliation of others;
  • hate speech or discrimination based on race, nationality, ethnic origin, religion, sex or gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or other characteristics;
  • content related to, or encouraging, illegal activity;
  • spam, advertising, scams, misleading links and repetitive bulk posts;
  • other people’s photos that you do not have the rights to publish;
  • impersonation of another person, brand or organisation;
  • other people’s personal data (addresses, phone numbers, documents, etc.) or photos of people in private settings without their consent.

You must also not get around other users’ blocks, create new accounts after being banned, abuse the reporting system, interfere with or hack the app, scrape data in bulk, or use bots.

7. Child safety

We do not tolerate any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and the sexualisation of minors. Such content is removed as soon as we find it, the account is permanently banned, and we report it to the relevant authorities as required by law. You can report such content with an in-app report (reason “Nudity or sexual content”) and by email to Ochizzza@gmail.com.

8. Reporting and blocking

  • Report. You can report any photo or user directly in the app (you need to be signed in). Reasons: spam or scam, nudity or sexual content, violence or dangerous acts, harassment or bullying, hate speech, someone else’s photo (copyright), something else.
  • Block. You can block any user (also when signed in). Posts from users you have blocked are hidden from you.
  • Automatic hiding. If three different people report a post, it is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.
  • Emergencies. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

9. Moderation and enforcement

We review reports and aim to do so within 24 hours. If content breaks these terms, we remove it and may remove the user who posted it from the service. Depending on the violation, we may:

  • remove a post or profile information;
  • issue a warning;
  • temporarily restrict or suspend the account;
  • permanently ban the account — without warning for serious violations;
  • report illegal content to the relevant authorities.

We do not review every post in advance and are not obliged to, but we may remove any content that breaks these terms. If you think your content was removed or your account blocked by mistake, write to Ochizzza@gmail.com and we will take another look.

10. Copyright complaints

If you believe a photo in the app infringes your copyright, report it in the app (reason “Someone else’s photo (copyright)”) or write to Ochizzza@gmail.com. Please include: the work that is infringed, where it is in the app (the author’s username and the photo title), your contact details, and a statement that you are the rights holder or are acting on their behalf. For catalog photos, we will check the source and licence and remove the photo if needed.
