Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data the SHOTBOOK mobile app for Android and iOS (the “app”) processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can control it.

In short

  • You can use the catalog, the camera guide and the “Repeat this shot” screen without an account.
  • Photos taken on the “Repeat this shot” screen are saved only to your device’s gallery. We never receive them.
  • If you create an account, we store your email, your profile and what you publish. Published photos and profiles are public.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers. We do not sell data.
  • You can delete your account and all related data in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.

2. What data we process

Without an account

  • Data on your device. Your list of saved photos and your chosen interface language are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Camera. The “Repeat this shot” screen uses the camera with your permission. The camera image is processed only on your device, and the photos you take are saved to your device’s gallery. We do not receive them and they are not uploaded.
  • Technical request data. When the app loads the catalog, the feed or images, the servers it contacts necessarily receive your device’s IP address and technical details of the request (time, requested address, client information). These may be written to server logs to keep the service running and secure. We do not use them to build a profile of you.
  • Views. When you open a photo from the feed, the app increases that photo’s total view counter. The counter stores only a total number and is not linked to who viewed the photo.

With an account

  • Account data: email address, password (stored only as a hash by our processor Supabase; we never see your password), an internal user ID, and the dates the account was created and last signed in.
  • Profile: username, display name and, if you choose, a bio and an avatar image. Your profile is visible to all app users.
  • Published content: the photos you upload, their title, description, category and the camera and editing settings you enter, the publication date and the view counter. Published content is visible to everyone and the image files are publicly accessible.
  • Saved photos: when you are signed in, your saved photos are also stored in your account so they are available on your other devices.
  • Reports and blocks: who filed a report, which photo or user it concerns, the chosen reason and the time; the list of users you have blocked.
  • Correspondence: if you email us, we receive your address and the content of your message.

Photo metadata. Image files can contain embedded metadata (EXIF) such as the camera model, the date taken and, if enabled on your device, the location where the photo was taken. If you do not want this information to become public together with the photo, remove it before publishing or turn off location tagging in your camera settings.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising identifiers.
  • The app contains no analytics SDKs, third-party trackers or behavioural tracking tools.
  • The app currently does not use any crash reporting service. If that changes, we will update this policy first.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts or other data that the app’s features do not need.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

5. Who we share data with

  • Supabase, Inc. is our data processor. Supabase provides authentication, the database and file storage, and processes data only on our instructions and to run the app.
  • Other users and anyone with the app. Your profile and published photos with their descriptions are public. We cannot control what others do with what they see (for example, take a screenshot).
  • Catalog photo sources. Catalog images are loaded from Flickr’s servers. As with loading any image from the internet, Flickr receives your device’s IP address and technical request data. No account data is sent to Flickr.
  • App stores. Google Play and the App Store process data about app downloads and updates under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities. We disclose data only when the law requires it or when it is necessary to protect people’s rights and safety, for example when reporting illegal content.
  • Change of ownership. If the app is transferred to another developer, data may pass to them under the terms of this policy; we will tell you in advance.

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers or analytics services.

6. Where data is stored

Account data and published content are stored in Supabase’s cloud infrastructure. This policy does not name a specific hosting region, so your data may be stored and processed outside your country, including in countries whose data protection laws differ from yours. Where the law requires additional safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the mechanisms provided in our data processing agreement with Supabase, such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, you have the right to:

  • access — find out what data we process about you and get a copy;
  • rectification — correct inaccurate data (you can edit your profile directly in the app);
  • erasure — delete your account and data (in the app or on request);
  • portability — receive the data you provided in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • object and restrict — object to processing based on legitimate interest, or ask us to restrict processing temporarily;
  • withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • complain — lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country or country of residence.

To exercise these rights, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, preferably from the email address registered with your account. To protect your data, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We reply within 30 days; if the law allows that period to be extended, we will tell you so and why.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 or the minimum age required in your country (16 in some EEA countries). Only users who have reached that age may create an account and publish photos. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

10. Security

Data travels between the app and the servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are stored by Supabase only as hashes. Access to data in the database is restricted by rules that let users change only their own records. Even so, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the supervisory authorities where the law requires it.
