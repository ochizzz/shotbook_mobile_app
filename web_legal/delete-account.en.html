Delete your account

App: SHOTBOOK
Developer: {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}
Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

You can delete your SHOTBOOK account and its data in two ways: directly in the app (immediately) or by email (within 30 days).

Option 1. In the app

  1. Open the SHOTBOOK app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. In the confirmation dialog, type the word it asks for and tap Delete.

Your account and data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2. By email

Use this if you no longer have the app or cannot sign in.

  1. Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address registered with your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Удаление аккаунта SHOTBOOK / SHOTBOOK account deletion.
  3. In the message, give your SHOTBOOK username and ask us to delete your account.

We process requests within 30 days and will reply when your account has been deleted. If the email does not come from the account’s address, we will ask you to confirm that the account is yours — this protects accounts from requests by other people.

What is deleted

  • your account: email address, password and user ID;
  • your profile: username, display name, bio and avatar;
  • all photos you published and their files, together with titles, descriptions, categories, settings and view counts;
  • saved photos stored in your account;
  • reports you filed;
  • your list of blocked users.

What may be kept, and for how long

DataRetention
Server backupsUp to 30 days after deletion, after which the data is gone from backups as well
Data we must keep by law (for example, records of content reported as illegal)As long as the law requires, and only to the extent necessary
Correspondence about your deletion requestAs long as needed to confirm the request was carried out

Data stored only on your device (the local list of saved photos, your chosen language) is not on our servers. To remove it, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Photos you took on the “Repeat this shot” screen stay in your device’s gallery — delete them there if you wish.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Delete a single post in the app: in your profile, press and hold the photo, then choose Delete post.
  • Edit or remove your bio and avatar in your profile.
  • Remove photos from your saved list, and unblock users in Settings.
  • To delete other specific data, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
